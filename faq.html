Frequently Asked Questions

What is Netflix?
Netflix is a streaming service that offers a wide variety of movies, TV shows, documentaries and more.
How much does Netflix cost?
Netflix offers different plans at different prices. Choose the plan that works best for you.
Where can I watch?
You can watch Netflix on smart TVs, smartphones, tablets, laptops, streaming devices and more.

You can cancel your Netflix membership anytime from your account settings.
What can I watch on Netflix?
Netflix has movies, TV shows, anime, documentaries and Netflix Originals across many genres.
Is Netflix good for kids?
Netflix includes a dedicated kids experience with age-appropriate content and parental controls.
